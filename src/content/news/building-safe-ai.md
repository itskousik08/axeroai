# Building Safe AI: Our Core Philosophy

At AXEROAI, we believe that the development of artificial general intelligence (AGI) is one of the most consequential endeavors in human history. With this belief comes a profound responsibility to ensure that the systems we build are safe, controllable, and aligned with human values.

## Safety as a First-Class Citizen

In many engineering disciplines, safety is an afterthought—a layer added at the end of the development cycle. In AI, this approach is insufficient. We treat safety as a first-class citizen, integrating it into the very foundation of our models.

### Constitutional AI

One of our core innovations is Constitutional AI. Instead of relying solely on human feedback to steer model behavior, we provide the model with a set of principles—a "constitution"—that it must follow. The model then uses these principles to evaluate its own responses and refine its behavior.

### Interpretability Research

We are also deeply invested in mechanistic interpretability. We want to understand not just *what* a model does, but *why* it does it. By peering into the "black box" of neural networks, we can identify and mitigate potential risks before they manifest.

## The Path Forward

The journey toward safe AGI is long and complex. It requires a combination of technical innovation, ethical reflection, and global cooperation. We are committed to sharing our research and collaborating with the broader community to ensure a future where AI is a force for good.
