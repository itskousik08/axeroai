# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2024

AxeroAI is committed to the highest standards of data security and system integrity. Because we build autonomous systems, we understand that trust is the foundation of our engineering.

## Data Ethics

We do not sell user data. Our business model is based on the success of our products, not the exploitation of user information.

### Encryption

All data within our ecosystem is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols.

### Autonomous Systems

Our autonomous agents operate within strict sandboxes to ensure no unauthorized data access occurs during processing.
